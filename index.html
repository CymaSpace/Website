Our Foundation

What is CymaSpace?

We combine scientific study, Deaf culture, community advocacy, and emerging hardware to build a more equitable, vibrant world.

♿

Accessibility & Inclusion

A leading advocate for DHH accessibility in performing arts, live venues, festivals, and broadcasting using Open Captioning, visual lighting, and ASL artists.

💡

Technology & Arts Incubator

Incubating visionary creators and hardware concepts—from sound-reactive LED arrays to vibrohaptic staging that lets people physically feel musical textures.

🎓

Education & Outreach

Empowering youth, performers, and community partners through programs like SignKids, ASL workshops, and hands-on makerspace labs.

✴

Cymatic Study

An epicenter for the study of Cymatics—translating sonic waves into visible, tangible phenomena so everyone can participate in the universal power of sound.

Woodstock Cafe ("Sign Language Cafe")

Owned and operated by CymaSpace as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, Woodstock Cafe is Portland's Deaf-centered community space for connection, creativity, and accessible conversation. Enjoy delicious espresso, community game nights, Sign Squad, and accessible arts events.

Location: 4103 SE Woodstock Blvd, Portland, OR

Programs: Sign Squad (Tuesdays), Sidelined Signers (Wednesdays)

SignKids: Bridging Communication Through Art

SignKids addresses language deprivation by creating an immersive, joyful ASL learning environment for Deaf and Hard of Hearing youth and their families. Enriched with storytelling, puppetry, Deaf mentorship, and hands-on creative play.

Focus: Natural ASL acquisition, cultural pride & family bonding

Sessions: Small cohorts with Deaf mentors and full ADA accessibility
