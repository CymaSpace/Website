Our Team & Leadership

CymaSpace is proudly governed and operated by Deaf and Hard of Hearing artists, technologists, educators, and community advocates dedicated to universal accessibility.

Operations & Creative Staff

Myles de Bastion

Founder & Creative Director

Myles is a User Interaction & Product Designer, Artistic Director, Musician, and Creative-Altruist. Born with progressive hearing loss, he develops cymatic technology and art installations that enable sound to be experienced as light, motion, and vibration. His work has appeared at the Oregon Museum of Science & Industry (OMSI), Portland Art Museum, on the Jimmy Kimmel Live! Show, and in concert installations for Grammy-winning jazz bassist Esperanza Spalding.

Chris Balduc

Development Director & Grant Writer

Deaf since age 13, Chris holds an MA in Special Education from CSU Northridge and has devoted his career to advocacy, social work, and technical drafting. He leads our foundation outreach, serves as a docent at the Portland Art Museum, and founded the First Thursday DHH Gallery Walk in Portland's Pearl District.

Mayan Fogarty

Hardware & Systems Technician

Mayan is a hard-of-hearing technician specializing in hardware troubleshooting, systems administration, and server maintenance. He keeps CymaSpace's computer lab, sound test benches, and digital infrastructure running smoothly for our community creators.
