Help Shape SUMA’s App, and Get a $25 Gift Card!

Published: July 3, 2025 • CymaSpace Community Archive

CymaSpace is partnering with SUMA, a Portland-based nonprofit that uses community power to help low-income households save money on the things they buy the most. SUMA is currently testing the visual design of their app and they’re asking for feedback from real people in our community, especially from folks who rely on clear, intuitive interfaces.

We’re looking for people who can spare 30 minutes to:

  • Tap through the app’s interface and icons
  • Evaluate readability: fonts, contrast, clarity
  • Give feedback on touch cues, icon logic, and your first impressions
  • Share what works and what feels confusing or missing

As a thank-you, SUMA will give you a $25 gift card.

This is part of a grassroots accessibility and usability audit that will help shape the future of a community-focused app. You don’t need to be a tech expert, your natural reaction is exactly what we’re looking for.

Schedule a 30 minute session below!
