Associated Press Features Woodstock Cafe: Portland’s Deaf-Led Sign Language Coffeehouse

January 5, 2026 • By Associated Press (AP News) • ASL Community & Culture

In a national feature syndicated across the United States, the Associated Press (AP News) highlighted CymaSpace’s Woodstock Cafe (“The Sign Language Cafe” at 4103 SE Woodstock Blvd), celebrating its role as a cherished sanctuary for Portland’s Deaf and Hard of Hearing community and a beacon of inclusive design.

Operating under the stewardship of nonprofit CymaSpace, the café is staffed predominantly by Deaf individuals and uses American Sign Language (ASL) as its native everyday language. For non-signing patrons, the café seamlessly incorporates accessibility technology: a microphone at the register transcribes spoken orders in real time onto a customer-facing display screen, alongside traditional menu boards and pen-and-paper options.

A Stable Rock for Employment & Belonging

In the AP report, co-founder Andre Gray described the café as more than just a place to grab morning coffee – it is an economic and social “rock” providing stable, welcoming employment in an environment free of communication barriers. Several staff members relocated from other states specifically to work in an ASL-centric establishment where Deaf culture is celebrated.

Beyond daily operations, Woodstock Cafe has blossomed into a bustling cultural hub. Every Tuesday evening, it hosts the widely popular “Sign Squad” meetup, bringing together Deaf individuals, ASL students, and allies for open conversation and cultural exchange, as well as community board game nights, poetry jams, and educational workshops.

The feature also emphasized CymaSpace's broader ten-year trajectory pioneering sensory substitution technologies, haptic platforms, and visual music installations, demonstrating how cultural gathering spaces and technology development go hand-in-hand to build Deaf empowerment.

To learn more about the café's hours and weekly event schedule, check our Events Calendar or visit the café at 4103 SE Woodstock Blvd in Portland.
