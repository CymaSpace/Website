Events Archive

A retrospective look at past live performances, art exhibitions, accessible concerts, and community workshops produced or supported by CymaSpace.

October 2025

Battery Powered Instruments Jam Session

An open, acoustic-electronic jam session where artists brought portable synths, drum machines, and acoustic instruments hooked into real-time sound-reactive lighting visualizers at the cafe.

Music & Tech Woodstock Cafe
November 2024

SignKids at Woodstock Sign Language Cafe

A special afternoon connecting young signers, KODAs, and Deaf parents with puppet storytelling, book readings, and interactive ASL games hosted at our community cafe space.

Youth Outreach Family Workshop
June 2019

CymaSpace at Wilsonville Festival of Arts

CymaSpace exhibited A Cymatic Experience at the 2019 Wilsonville Festival of Arts, presenting tactile furniture, responsive lighting, and ASL docents to thousands of festival attendees.

Public Exhibition Outdoor Festival
June 2019

Interpreted Event: Northwest Queer Chorus Pride Concert

Full ASL interpretation and sound-to-light stage lighting for the Northwest Queer Chorus Pride Concert, bringing messages of hope, queer empowerment, and deaf inclusion to Portland audiences.

Concert Access Live Performance
Ongoing Series

First Thursday DHH Gallery Walks

Led by development director and PAM docent Chris Balduc, an accessible art appreciation walking group guiding Deaf and Hard of Hearing community members through Portland's Pearl District art galleries.

Cultural Tour Pearl District
Museum Exhibition

OMSI "Guitars: Science of Sound" Exhibition

CymaSpace exhibited the #seeingsounds LED interactive piano and tactile floor units in the central foyer of OMSI, welcoming thousands of school groups and visitors over a multi-month residency.

Museum Residency OMSI Portland
