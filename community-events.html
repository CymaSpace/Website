Woodstock Cafe Events

Owned and operated by CymaSpace as an Oregon 501(c)(3) nonprofit, Woodstock Cafe—affectionately known as the "Sign Language Cafe"—is the gathering space for the Deaf and hearing communities to relax, converse, and celebrate ASL and accessible arts.

Weekly Series & Social Meetups

Tuesdays • 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM

Sign Squad Weekly ASL Meetup

Join our flagship silent meetup! Meet new people, practice signing, learn new signs, share stories, and build community in an inclusive, voice-off environment. All fluency levels welcome, from fluent signers to curious beginners.

Voice-Off Event Free
Wednesdays • 5:00 PM - 7:00 PM

Sidelined Signers

Ever felt nervous about signing in a fast-paced conversation? Sidelined Signers is designed specifically for beginner and intermediate signers who want low-pressure practice and encouragement to step into active ASL dialog.

Beginner / Intermediate Free
1st & 3rd Thursdays • 6:00 PM - 8:30 PM

ASL Game Night

Board games, party games, cards, and trivia played in ASL! A lively, fun-filled night where friendly competition brings smiles and lots of expressive storytelling.

All Ages Welcome Free

Special Events & Fundraisers

Fundraiser
Monthly Event • 10:00 AM - 3:15 PM

Community Bake Sale & Craft Market

Help keep the lights on, the community connected, and the heart of this space beating strong. Enjoy delicious traditional, vegan, gluten-free, sugar-free, and keto pastries made by local Deaf bakers. Proceeds support cafe operations and Deaf outreach.

Supports 501(c)(3) Nonprofit Donate to Cafe Fund
Tech Workshop
Quarterly Event • 2:00 PM - 5:00 PM

Program Your Own LED Light Show!

Hands-on creative coding and electronics workshop at the cafe! Learn how sound-reactive microcontrollers (Arduino / ESP32) can be programmed to translate beats and basslines into vibrant lighting sequences.
