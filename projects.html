Innovations & Creations

Our Projects & Initiatives

From sound-reactive acoustic pianos and vibrotactile furniture to youth language programs and award-winning ASL comedy films, explore how CymaSpace bridges the hearing and Deaf worlds through the science and art of cymatics.

Universal Design • Haptics

Universal Music Design (UMD)

Pioneering multi-sensory music accessibility through spatial haptics, wearable gesture instruments (Gestolumina), Woojer vest fleets, and Deaf-led studio production.

Explore Project → Visit UMD site →
Youth Program

SignKids: Arts, Play & ASL

A transformative program addressing language deprivation and strengthening bonds between Deaf parents, hearing children, and Deaf youth through creative storytelling and play.

Community Outreach Explore SignKids →
Hardware Tech

Audiolux One System

The first-ever effects unit and lighting controller for real-time sound-reactive visuals. Intelligently translates music into light, frequency color, and movement without programming.

Proprietary Device View Details →
Interactive Installation

#seeingsounds LED Piano for OMSI

A vintage acoustic piano retrofitted with an LED matrix front panel responding in real-time to pitch and dynamics. Exhibited at the Oregon Museum of Science & Industry.

Museum Exhibit View Story →
Stage Lighting

Modular Cymatic Triangles

Portable, lightweight sound-reactive geometric stage sculpture featured at PDX Pop Now! festival, accompanying dozens of live touring artists and bands.

Festival Installation View Details →
Tactile Sound

Vibrohaptic Tactile Furniture

Custom seating engineered with acoustic transducers that convert sound frequencies into physical tactile vibration, allowing visitors to feel music in full-body detail.

Haptic Engineering View Details →

A witty short comedy satire written and directed by Myles de Bastion poking fun at well-intentioned hearing inventors attempting to "fix" Deaf communication.
