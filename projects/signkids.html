SignKids: Bridging Communication & Cultivating Identity

An enriching, art-centric initiative designed to prevent language deprivation, build confidence in American Sign Language (ASL), and strengthen connections between Deaf parents, hearing children, and Deaf youth.

What is #SignKids?

SignKids addresses the critical challenge of language deprivation among Deaf and Hard of Hearing (DHH) children while strengthening bonds between Deaf parents and their hearing children (KODAs). Through art-centric activities, storytelling, games, and play, ASL is not treated merely as a classroom subject, but naturally acquired and celebrated in an inclusive linguistic setting.

Deaf children engage in dynamic activities with adult Deaf mentors, gaining cultural pride and role models. Hearing parents and siblings partake in this shared journey, breaking down communication barriers and fostering deep family connections.

What to Expect

  • Dedicated Deaf ASL mentors leading every session.
  • Hands-on creative art projects, puppet storytelling, and tactile games.
  • Welcoming orientation for first-time families.
  • Small cohorts (up to 4 families per session) ensuring individualized care.
  • Community kitchen & ADA accessible facilities.

Accessibility & Accommodations

  • Mobility: Wheelchair and mobility device ADA entrance on SE 18th Ave.
  • Open Captioning: Live transcription available for multi-lingual needs.
  • Scent-free: We maintain a fragrance-conscious facility for chemical sensitivities.
  • Custom Requests: Contact info@cymaspace.org in advance for personalized accommodations.
