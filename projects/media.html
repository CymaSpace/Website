🎬 CymaSpace Media Lab

Media, Cinema & Performance

Our Media Program equips Deaf filmmakers, storytellers, and performers with studio technology, green screens, and production support to craft original cultural cinema and visual music.

Comedy Satire Written & Directed by Myles de Bastion

"Mouth Language Device" an ASL Comedy Short

Well-intentioned hearing inventors have been trying for years to create gadgets, gloves, and translation devices to "help" Deaf people communicate—frequently ignoring what Deaf people actually want or need!

In this acclaimed comedy satire, the tables are turned: Deaf engineers reveal their revolutionary new invention to help hearing people communicate using a humorous mouth-actuated apparatus. Produced in close collaboration with the CymaSpace Media Program and local Deaf cast members.

Documentary Short Directed by Travis Howe (Red Lab Films)

DEAFNESS + MUSIC

A compelling short documentary featuring Myles de Bastion, the founder of CymaSpace and Audiolux Devices. The film explores Myles' personal journey with progressive hearing loss, his love of musical instruments, and the creative genesis behind turning sound waves into physical light and touch so others could experience what he envisioned.

Music Video Choreography & Performance

Hideaway: ASL & Dance Music Video

A high-energy collaborative music video pairing American Sign Language poetry with expressive contemporary dance, backed by sound-reactive LED backdrops developed in the CymaSpace makerspace.

Puppetry & Youth Series Featuring Korian "Koko" Thomas

This Deaf Puppet Needs a Name

Meet the newest personality at CymaSpace! Visual Description: Korian "Koko" Thomas, a Black DeafBlind disability advocate, hosts an engaging ASL video series introducing our custom character puppet. Through interactive community voting and children's participation, the series teaches expressive signing and tactile engagement.
